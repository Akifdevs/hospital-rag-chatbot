# The Completely Unnecessary Guide to Making a Midnight Sandwich

This recipe exists for no practical reason except that sometimes dinner happens at 12:43 a.m.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices of bread
- 1 spoonful of peanut butter
- 1 small apple
- A pinch of salt
- A little honey
- Black pepper, because apparently we're making decisions tonight
- Optional: crushed roasted peanuts

## Instructions

1. Toast the bread until it is slightly crisp but not aggressively crunchy.
2. Spread peanut butter across one slice.
3. Slice the apple very thinly and arrange the pieces in overlapping rows.
4. Drizzle a small amount of honey over the apple.
5. Add an extremely small pinch of salt.
6. Add black pepper. Stop before it becomes ridiculous.
7. Sprinkle crushed peanuts on top if available.
8. Close the sandwich and cut it diagonally, because diagonal sandwiches have better morale.

## Why Does This Work?

The peanut butter provides richness, the apple adds acidity and texture, while honey supplies sweetness. Salt makes the flavors louder, and black pepper adds a tiny amount of confusion.

The result is somewhere between breakfast, dessert, and a snack invented by someone who refused to go grocery shopping.

## Emergency Midnight Variant

If there are no apples, use banana.

If there is no peanut butter, use another nut or seed spread.

If there is no bread, reconsider your entire evening.

## Final Verdict

This sandwich is unnecessary, slightly strange, and surprisingly good.

Serve immediately while questioning your life choices.
